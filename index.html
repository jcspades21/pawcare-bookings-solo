Welcome to PawCare Bookings

PawCare Bookings is a browser-based application that allows pet owners to schedule grooming appointments online without needing to call or visit in person. Choose a service, pick an available time slot, and review your appointment request before submitting it.

How Booking Works

  1. Select the grooming service your pet needs.
  2. Choose an available date and time.
  3. Fill out your contact and pet details.
  4. Confirm your booking and get a booking ID right away.
  5. Email confirmations and reminders are coming in a later update.
